# Integrated study workspace: completion audit

Audit date: 2026-10-29. This audit covers the full requested objective: word-level editing, flexible display formats, coherent source management and location, a graph of stored notebook structure, one cohesive interface, and AI-driven learning personalization. Examples such as Canvas, Notion, NotebookLM and Obsidian informed the interaction patterns; the objective does not require replicas of those products. Tables satisfy the stated tables-or-nested-pages example; nested-page authoring is not claimed.
